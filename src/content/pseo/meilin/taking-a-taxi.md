To take a taxi in China, greet the driver with **师傅，你好** (*shīfu, nǐ hǎo*), then say **我要去……** (*wǒ yào qù…*, "I want to go to…") followed by the place. When you arrive, **就在这里停** (*jiù zài zhèlǐ tíng*, "stop right here") is the phrase you'll use most. Always keep the address in Chinese characters on your phone as a backup.

## Before you get in

Most people in Chinese cities book rides through **滴滴** (*Dīdī*), the ride-hailing app, which has an English interface. Street taxis are still common too. Either way, these phrases help:

- **打车** — *dǎchē* — "to take a taxi"
- **出租车** — *chūzūchē* — "taxi"
- **我叫了滴滴。** — *wǒ jiào le Dīdī* — "I ordered a DiDi."

## Greeting the driver and giving the destination

- **师傅，你好。** — *shīfu, nǐ hǎo* — "Hello, driver." *Shīfu* is the respectful way to address drivers and tradespeople.
- **我要去……** — *wǒ yào qù…* — "I want to go to…"
- **请到……** — *qǐng dào…* — "Please take me to…"
- **去这个地方。** — *qù zhège dìfang* — "To this place." Show the address on your phone.
- **去机场。** — *qù jīchǎng* — "To the airport."
- **去火车站。** — *qù huǒchēzhàn* — "To the train station."

Tone tip: *shīfu* starts high and flat. If you let the first syllable fall, it sounds like a different word. Keep *shī* level, like singing one note.

## Directions

- **往左拐。** — *wǎng zuǒ guǎi* — "Turn left."
- **往右拐。** — *wǎng yòu guǎi* — "Turn right."
- **一直走。** — *yìzhí zǒu* — "Go straight."
- **前面。** — *qiánmiàn* — "Up ahead."
- **在下一个路口。** — *zài xià yí ge lùkǒu* — "At the next intersection."
- **开慢一点。** — *kāi màn yìdiǎn* — "Drive a little slower, please."
- **还要多久？** — *hái yào duōjiǔ?* — "How much longer?"

Left and right are easy to mix up: *zuǒ* (左, left) dips and rises; *yòu* (右, right) falls sharply.

## Stopping and getting out

- **就在这里停。** — *jiù zài zhèlǐ tíng* — "Stop right here."
- **靠边停。** — *kàobiān tíng* — "Pull over."
- **在门口停。** — *zài ménkǒu tíng* — "Stop at the entrance."
- **到了。** — *dào le* — "We're here."

## Paying

- **多少钱？** — *duōshao qián?* — "How much?"
- **可以扫码吗？** — *kěyǐ sǎo mǎ ma?* — "Can I pay by scanning a code?"
- **需要发票。** — *xūyào fāpiào* — "I need a receipt." Useful for business trips.

DiDi rides are paid in the app, so you can simply say **谢谢师傅** (*xièxie shīfu*, "thanks, driver") and get out.

## Messaging your DiDi driver

Drivers often call or message when they arrive. These short texts are easy to copy:

- **我在门口。** — *wǒ zài ménkǒu* — "I'm at the entrance."
- **我马上到。** — *wǒ mǎshàng dào* — "I'll be right there."
- **我穿红色的衣服。** — *wǒ chuān hóngsè de yīfu* — "I'm wearing red."
- **我不会说中文，请发文字。** — *wǒ bú huì shuō Zhōngwén, qǐng fā wénzì* — "I don't speak Chinese, please text."

## A sample exchange

> **You:** 师傅，你好。去这个地方。 (*Shīfu, nǐ hǎo. Qù zhège dìfang.*) [shows phone]
> **Driver:** 好。 (*Hǎo.*)
> **You:** 在下一个路口往右拐。 (*Zài xià yí ge lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi.*)
> **Driver:** 好的。到了。 (*Hǎo de. Dào le.*)
> **You:** 就在这里停。可以扫码吗？ (*Jiù zài zhèlǐ tíng. Kěyǐ sǎo mǎ ma?*)

## FAQ

**How do you say "stop here" in Chinese?**
就在这里停 — *jiù zài zhèlǐ tíng* — or simply 这里停 — *zhèlǐ tíng*.

**How do you address a taxi driver in Chinese?**
师傅 — *shīfu*. It's respectful and friendly.

**How do you say "I want to go to…" in Mandarin?**
我要去…… — *wǒ yào qù…* — followed by the place.

**Can I use DiDi without speaking Chinese?**
Yes. The app has an English version and can translate messages, but a few phrases make pick-ups much smoother.

**How do you say "turn left" and "turn right" in Chinese?**
往左拐 — *wǎng zuǒ guǎi* (left) and 往右拐 — *wǎng yòu guǎi* (right).

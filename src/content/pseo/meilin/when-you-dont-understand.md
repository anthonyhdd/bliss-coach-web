When you don't understand someone in Chinese, say **我听不懂** (*wǒ tīng bu dǒng*, "I don't understand what I'm hearing"), then **请说慢一点** (*qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn*, "please speak a little slower"). These two phrases keep a conversation alive instead of ending it with a shrug — and native speakers are almost always happy to slow down.

## "I don't understand"

Mandarin distinguishes between not understanding what you hear and what you read.

- **我听不懂。** — *wǒ tīng bu dǒng* — "I don't understand" (listening).
- **我看不懂。** — *wǒ kàn bu dǒng* — "I can't read/understand this" (reading, like a menu or a sign).
- **我不明白。** — *wǒ bù míngbai* — "I don't get it" — the idea or explanation.
- **我没听清楚。** — *wǒ méi tīng qīngchu* — "I didn't catch that clearly."

Tone tip: in *tīng bu dǒng*, the middle *bu* is short and toneless. Rushing through it sounds natural; stressing it sounds robotic.

## Asking them to repeat or slow down

- **请再说一遍。** — *qǐng zài shuō yí biàn* — "Please say it again."
- **请说慢一点。** — *qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn* — "Please speak a bit slower."
- **你能再说一次吗？** — *nǐ néng zài shuō yí cì ma?* — "Could you say that once more?"
- **不好意思，什么？** — *bù hǎo yìsi, shénme?* — "Sorry, what?"

Adding **不好意思** (*bù hǎo yìsi*, "sorry / excuse me") at the start makes any request sound softer.

## Asking what something means

- **这是什么意思？** — *zhè shì shénme yìsi?* — "What does this mean?"
- **……是什么意思？** — *… shì shénme yìsi?* — "What does … mean?"
- **怎么写？** — *zěnme xiě?* — "How do you write it?"
- **能写下来吗？** — *néng xiě xiàlái ma?* — "Could you write it down?" Very useful for names and addresses.
- **用中文怎么说？** — *yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō?* — "How do you say it in Chinese?"

## Explaining your level

- **我的中文不太好。** — *wǒ de Zhōngwén bú tài hǎo* — "My Chinese isn't very good."
- **我在学中文。** — *wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén* — "I'm learning Chinese." This often earns patience and a smile.
- **你会说英语吗？** — *nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma?* — "Do you speak English?"

## When you finally get it

- **我明白了！** — *wǒ míngbai le!* — "I get it now!"
- **懂了！** — *dǒng le!* — "Got it!"
- **谢谢你的耐心。** — *xièxie nǐ de nàixīn* — "Thanks for your patience."

## A sample exchange

> **Shopkeeper:** 这个要现在付还是到时候付？ (*Zhège yào xiànzài fù háishi dào shíhou fù?*)
> **You:** 不好意思，我听不懂。请说慢一点。 (*Bù hǎo yìsi, wǒ tīng bu dǒng. Qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn.*)
> **Shopkeeper:** 现在……付钱？还是……以后？ (*Xiànzài… fù qián? Háishi… yǐhòu?*)
> **You:** 哦，明白了！现在付。 (*Ò, míngbai le! Xiànzài fù.*)

## Why these phrases matter

Most learners stop speaking the moment they miss a word. These phrases turn that moment into practice: every "please say it again" is another chance to hear real Mandarin at a speed you can handle.

## FAQ

**How do you say "I don't understand" in Chinese?**
我听不懂 — *wǒ tīng bu dǒng* — when listening; 我看不懂 — *wǒ kàn bu dǒng* — when reading.

**How do you ask someone to speak slowly in Mandarin?**
请说慢一点 — *qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn*.

**How do you say "please repeat" in Chinese?**
请再说一遍 — *qǐng zài shuō yí biàn*.

**What is the difference between 不懂 and 不明白?**
不懂 (*bù dǒng*) is general "don't understand"; 不明白 (*bù míngbai*) is closer to "I don't get the idea or explanation."

**How do you ask what a word means in Chinese?**
Say the word followed by 是什么意思 — *shì shénme yìsi?*

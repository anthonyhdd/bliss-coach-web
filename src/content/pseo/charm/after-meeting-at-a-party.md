Short answer: text within a day, remind them who you are, and reference something specific from the night. "Hey, it's Sam from Jess's party — the one who lost the ping-pong tournament with great dignity. Really fun talking with you." Then, in the next few messages, suggest a plan.

## What's actually going on

Parties are loud, busy, and full of new faces. The person you met may have exchanged numbers with a few people, had a couple of drinks, and remembered you mostly as "the guy by the snacks." Your first text has two jobs:

1. **Identify yourself** so they don't have to guess.
2. **Bring back the good feeling** from your conversation.

The specific detail does both. A shared joke, the song that was playing, the terrible punch — anything that says "we had a moment" instead of "we exchanged numbers."

The window matters. The spark from a party fades quickly. A text the next day keeps it alive; a text a week later feels like an afterthought.

## What NOT to send

- **"Hey"** with no name. They may have no idea who you are.
- **"Hey it's me from last night."** Which one?
- **Anything about how drunk either of you were.** Even if funny, it can embarrass them.
- **A long message** recapping the whole night.
- **A text at 3am on the way home.** It reads as a late-night impulse, not interest.
- **Immediately asking for a hookup.** If that's the vibe you both had, they'll let you know.

## Copy-ready texts

### Remind them who you are

> Hey, it's Tom from Jess's party — the one who argued pineapple belongs on pizza. Still standing by it. Great meeting you!

> Hi! Sam here, we talked about Lisbon by the kitchen at Alex's party. Still thinking about that pastry place you recommended.

> Hey, it's Chris — we bonded over hating that playlist last night. Glad I got your number.

### Playful follow-up on an inside joke

> I've been practising my dance moves since last night. Prepare to be impressed next time.

> Just confirming: you still owe me a rematch at beer pong.

> I looked up the answer to our argument. You were right. I'm not happy about it.

### Move toward a plan

> I had a great time talking with you. Want to continue the conversation over a drink this week?

> You mentioned you've never tried that ramen place — want to fix that on Thursday?

> Let's do this without a DJ drowning us out. Coffee on Saturday?

### If they text first

> Hey! So glad you reached out. Last night was fun — you were the best part of it.

### If you're not sure they remember you

> Hi, this is Ben — we met at Mia's party on Saturday (I was wearing the terrible yellow jacket). Just wanted to say it was great talking with you.

A visual detail helps them place you.

## Timing

- **The next day, late morning to evening,** is ideal. Early enough to keep the spark, late enough that nobody is hungover and grumpy.
- **Same night is okay** only if it's a short "Great meeting you, get home safe!" — not an attempt to keep the night going.
- **Suggest a plan within the first few exchanges.** Long text-only chats after a party often fizzle.

## When to stop

If they don't reply within a couple of days, you can send one light follow-up ("No worries if you're busy — would still love to grab that drink"). If there's still nothing, let it go.

If they do reply, keep momentum: make a concrete plan rather than chatting for a week. ## FAQ

**How soon should you text someone you met at a party?**
The next day is best. Within 24–48 hours keeps the memory of your conversation fresh.

**What should the first text say?**
Your name, where you met, and one specific detail from your conversation — then a friendly line that makes it easy to reply.

**Is it okay to text the same night?**
A short "great meeting you" is fine. Save the real conversation for the next day.

**What if they gave me their number but don't reply?**
Send one light follow-up after a couple of days. If there's still no answer, move on — people give out numbers for many reasons.

**Should I text or DM on Instagram?**
If you got their number, text. If you only followed each other, a DM is fine — see [what to send after following on Instagram](/charm/what-to-text/after-following-on-instagram/).

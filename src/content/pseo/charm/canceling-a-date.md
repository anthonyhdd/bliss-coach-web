Short answer: cancel as early as you can, say sorry once, and — if you still want to see them — offer a specific new time in the same message. "I'm so sorry, something came up at work and I can't make tonight. Could we do Thursday at 7 instead? I was really looking forward to it."

## What's actually going on

A cancellation is only a problem when it leaves the other person guessing. They'll ask themselves: *is this a real reason, or a polite way out?* Your text should answer that question.

The single strongest signal that you're still interested is a **specific** new plan. "Let's reschedule sometime" sounds like a soft no. "Thursday at 7?" sounds like you mean it.

There are really three situations:

- **You want to see them, but something came up.** Apologise, give a brief reason, propose a new time.
- **You're not interested anymore.** Cancel honestly and don't reschedule.
- **It's very last minute.** Apologise more, explain a bit more, and consider a call.

## What NOT to send

- **"Can't make it tonight."** Nothing else. Reads as uninterested.
- **"Something came up"** with no new plan. Sounds like an excuse.
- **A long elaborate story.** Over-explaining makes people doubt the reason.
- **A cancellation 20 minutes before** when you knew in the morning.
- **Fake rescheduling** when you're not interested — it only delays the no.
- **Cancelling by not showing up.** Never.

## Copy-ready texts

### Cancelling and rescheduling

> I'm really sorry, but I have to cancel tonight — my flight got moved. Could we do Saturday instead? I was looking forward to this.

> Bad news: I'm stuck at work late. I hate cancelling. Are you free Wednesday? Drinks on me to make up for it.

> I've come down with something and don't want to pass it on. Rain check for next week — Tuesday or Wednesday work for you?

> Family thing came up that I can't move. So sorry! How about Sunday brunch instead?

### Very last minute

> I'm so sorry for the short notice — my car broke down on the way. Can I call you in a few minutes to explain and find another night?

> I feel terrible cancelling this late. [Brief real reason.] I'd love to make it up to you — dinner Friday, my treat?

### When you're not interested anymore

> Hey, I need to cancel our plans for Saturday. I've thought about it and I don't feel a romantic connection, and I didn't want to waste your time. I'm sorry, and I wish you the best.

> I'm going to cancel tomorrow — I've realised I'm not in a place to date right now. Sorry for the late notice, and thank you for understanding.

Only say "not in a place to date" if it's true.

### When you need to move the time, not the day

> Could we push to 8 instead of 7? Work is running late. Still very much on!

> Would you mind if we started a bit later tonight? 7:30 instead of 7?

### After they reply kindly

> Thanks for being so understanding. Looking forward to Thursday!

## Timing

- **Cancel as soon as you know.** Morning is better than afternoon; the day before is better than the day of.
- **Under two hours before the date?** A call or voice note is more respectful than a text.
- **Propose the new date in the same message.** Don't make them ask.

## When to stop

One apology is enough. If they accept the new time, confirm it the day before (see [how to confirm a date over text](/charm/what-to-text/confirming-a-date/)) and show up.

Avoid cancelling twice. A second cancellation, especially a vague one, usually ends things. ## FAQ

**How do you cancel a date without seeming uninterested?**
Apologise, give a short real reason, and propose a specific new day and time in the same message.

**Is it rude to cancel a date over text?**
No, if you give reasonable notice. For very last-minute cancellations, a quick call is more considerate.

**How much notice should you give when cancelling a date?**
As much as possible — ideally a day. Same-day cancellations should come with a clear apology and a new plan.

**What's a good excuse to cancel a date?**
The real one, stated briefly. Made-up excuses tend to show, and over-explaining makes people suspicious.

**How do I cancel a date if I'm not interested anymore?**
Be honest and kind, don't offer to reschedule, and keep it short: thank them and say you don't feel a romantic connection.

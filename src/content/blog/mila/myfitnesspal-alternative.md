The right MyFitnessPal alternative depends on why you're leaving. If it's the ads, the paywalled barcode scanner, or the tedium of manual logging, plenty of trackers do the same job more cleanly. But if you're leaving because logging every bite never actually changed your habits, the fix isn't another database — it's a coach. An AI nutrition coach like Mila talks with you about your eating instead of asking you to catalog it.

## Why people look for a MyFitnessPal alternative

Two very different reasons, and they point to different tools:

1. **"The app got worse / tedious."** Ads, features moved behind the paywall, endless manual entry. You want a *better tracker* — cleaner, cheaper, less friction.
2. **"Tracking didn't work for me."** You logged diligently for weeks, then stopped, and nothing changed. You don't need a better spreadsheet of food — you need accountability and help with the habits.

Be honest about which one you are. It changes the answer completely.

**Stay on a tracker (MyFitnessPal or a lighter clone) if** you genuinely like logging and just want it cleaner or cheaper. The database is the moat, and detailed loggers do get results from it.

**Pick an AI nutrition coach (like [Mila](/mila/)) if** you've done the logging thing and it didn't stick. ## FAQ

**Is there a free MyFitnessPal alternative?**
Yes, several — including lighter trackers and coaching apps like Mila that let you try before subscribing. Match the tool to your reason for leaving: a cleaner tracker if you like logging, a coach if logging never stuck.

**What's better than MyFitnessPal for actually changing habits?**
For habit change specifically, a coach beats a logger. An AI nutrition coach you can talk to addresses cravings, excuses and consistency — the reasons diets fail — which pure calorie tracking doesn't touch.

**Do I still need to count calories with a coaching app?**
Not necessarily. Coaching apps focus on the behavior around food rather than requiring you to log every item, though you can still track if you find it motivating.

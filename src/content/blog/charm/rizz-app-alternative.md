## Canned lines vs a coach for your situation

Pickup-line generators have one move: give them a prompt, get a "smooth" line. The problems show up fast — the lines are generic, they don't fit the person you're talking to, and matches have seen them before. Worse, they don't teach you anything, so you're dependent forever.

A coach reads the real context — the vibe, what they last said, the situation you're in — and helps you send something that sounds like you. Over time you also just get better at it, which is the actual goal.

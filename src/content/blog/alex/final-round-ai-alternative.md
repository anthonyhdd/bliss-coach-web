The main choice among Final Round AI alternatives is between a **live-interview copilot** — a tool that feeds you suggested answers *during* the real interview — and a **private mock interviewer** you rehearse with beforehand until you don't need help on the day. Final Round AI is known for the live-assist angle; if you'd rather walk in genuinely prepared (and avoid the risk of relying on an on-screen assistant mid-call), a rehearsal-first app like Alex is the better fit.

## The real fork: assist live, or rehearse first

Live copilots read the interviewer's question and surface talking points in real time. It's impressive in a demo, but it has three problems in practice: it can be obvious you're reading, many interviews are in-person or on locked-down platforms where it won't help, and it teaches you nothing you keep for the next interview.

Rehearsal-first tools flip it: you do the hard reps *before* the interview, out loud, until your answers are tight and automatic. Nothing to hide, nothing to depend on, and the skill transfers to every future round.

## Who should pick which

**Pick a live copilot if** you want in-the-moment support and you're interviewing on a platform where an on-screen assistant is workable — accepting the risk that it reads as coached.

**Pick a rehearsal-first mock interviewer (like [Alex](/alex/)) if** you'd rather be the kind of candidate who doesn't need help mid-interview. On the day, you just answer.

## FAQ

**Is Final Round AI worth it?**
If you specifically want live in-interview assistance and your format allows it, it does that. If your goal is to be prepared enough not to need help on the call, a rehearsal-first mock interviewer is a better match for the money.

**Is there a Final Round AI alternative for practicing beforehand?**
Yes — mock-interview apps like Alex are built for rehearsal: a full back-and-forth interview you run before the real one, with coaching on your answers rather than prompts during the call.

**Can AI really help me prepare for interviews?**
For preparation, very much — a good AI mock interviewer gives you unlimited realistic reps and honest feedback, which is exactly what most candidates lack. The useful version rehearses you beforehand rather than assisting you live.

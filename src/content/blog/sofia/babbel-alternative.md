The best Babbel alternative depends on whether your problem is *knowing* Spanish or *speaking* it. Babbel's structured, grammar-forward lessons are strong for building a foundation and understanding how the language works. But many learners finish lessons and still freeze in a real conversation — because recognizing the right answer isn't the same as producing it out loud. If that's you, a speaking-first AI tutor like Sofia targets the exact gap Babbel leaves.

## The gap most learners hit

Structured courses teach you to *recognize* — fill the blank, pick the ending, match the phrase. Speaking requires you to *produce* — build a sentence in real time while someone waits. These are different skills, and lesson-based apps train mostly the first one.

That's why "I did Babbel for months and still can't hold a conversation" is such a common story. The lessons weren't wrong; they just weren't reps of the thing you actually want to do.

Yes — several, including AI tutors like Sofia that let you have real conversations before subscribing. Pick based on your gap: structure if you're starting out, speaking practice if you're stuck.

For speaking specifically, an AI tutor you converse with gives more of the reps that matter — producing sentences out loud with instant correction. Babbel is stronger as a structured foundation than as speaking practice.
